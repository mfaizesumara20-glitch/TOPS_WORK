What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving a website so that it appears higher in search engine results like Google.

- How SEO works:
Keywords – Use words people search for.
Quality content – Provide useful and relevant information.
On-page SEO – Optimize titles, headings, images, and content.
Backlinks – Get links from other trusted websites.
Technical SEO – Make the website fast, mobile-friendly, and easy for search engines to understand.

**Example:**
